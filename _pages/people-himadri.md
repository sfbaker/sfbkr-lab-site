A virology enthusiast from West Bengal, India. In his free time, Himadri finds solace in walking amidst green spaces, and enjoys movies and music.

Himadri pursued his doctoral research at CSIR-Indian Institute of Chemical Biology, Kolkata, studying clinical dengue viruses and the serological cross-reactivity between dengue and SARS-CoV-2. At the Baker Lab, he studies influenza virus and its intricate interactions with its host.
